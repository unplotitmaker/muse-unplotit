1. Upload your chart

Drop a chart image here, paste it, or browse

Ctrl/⌘+V pastes a screenshot straight from your clipboard.

2. Calibrate the chart

Drag on the image to draw a rectangle around the plot’s data region (the area the line actually travels in). Corners resize it, dragging inside moves it, and the dashed orange line is the legend guard — drag it below any legend swatch or title sitting inside the plot. Then fill in the axis values and date range below.

Plot area (pixels)

Tip: zoom your browser in for precise placement; the fields let you nudge exact pixels.

Legend guard

Pixels above this line are ignored — use it to exclude a legend swatch. The true line must never go above it; it defaults to the rectangle top.
